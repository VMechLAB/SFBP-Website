02 — ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is SFBP?

SFBP (Shark Fin Biometric Plane) is a student engineering project at PTŠ Kruševac. We’re developing a flying-wing aircraft with modular equipment space. The aircraft design is still in development, and a 1:10 scale model has been built.

  • Flying wing: equipment is carried within the aircraft itself.
  • Interchangeable equipment: one aircraft can be adapted for different tasks.
  • We’re starting with the L1000 and checking the basic design assumptions.

03 — CHALLENGES

The engineering challenges

01

Flight time

Weight and power use affect how long an aircraft can stay aloft.

02

Different equipment

Imaging, measurements and experiments call for different equipment.

03

Weight and space

Equipment has to fit without pushing the aircraft over its target weight.

04

System integration

Electronics, sensors and flight controls need to work together.

04 — OUR APPROACH

One aircraft, interchangeable equipment

01

Shared airframe

We’re developing one airframe as the basis for future configurations.

02

Interchangeable equipment

The plan is to change equipment to suit the task. The interfaces are still being developed.

03

Step-by-step development

We’re starting with a basic model and checking each step before moving on.

04

Solar power research

We’re looking into supplementary solar power. It hasn’t been built or tested.
